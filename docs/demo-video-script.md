# Demo video: script + shot list (about 2 minutes)

**Title idea:** "I built Iron Man's JARVIS as a college student (it runs my PC, holograms, and a police field assistant)"

## Before you record

- [ ] Jarvis running; PC browser at https://localhost:3900 (certificate trusted, no warning).
- [ ] Phone on the same Wi-Fi with Jarvis installed; screen recording on (Android: Quick Settings → Screen record).
- [ ] PC screen recorder: Windows **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS.
- [ ] Good light on your face and hands for HOLO; plain background.
- [ ] Demo incident "DEMO — Two-car crash" open in a second tab.
- [ ] Quiet room; say "Hey Jarvis" clearly.

## Shot list

| Time | Show | Say (voice-over or to camera) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | You at your desk: "Hey Jarvis, good morning." The orb answers with the briefing. | "This is Jarvis. I built it from scratch, and it runs on my own PC." |
| 0:08–0:20 | "Hey Jarvis, play lofi on YouTube" → opens. "Volume down." "Pause." | "Everyday commands answer instantly. Everything else goes to Claude with live web search." |
| 0:20–0:45 | Click **HOLO**. Grab an orb, throw it, stretch one with two hands, crash two together. Say "Jarvis, make it red." | "Hand tracking turns my webcam into Tony Stark's hologram table. Crash two orbs and they blow up." |
| 0:45–0:52 | HOLO tags your face with your name. | "It recognizes me, all inside the browser. My face never leaves the PC, and it never unlocks anything." |
| 0:52–1:30 | Phone: open **Field Assist**, demo crash. Hands-free: "Jarvis, note: the road was wet." "Jarvis, take a photo of the bumper." Tap **Draft report**; scroll the draft and the "Missing" list. | "The part I'm proudest of: a hands-free assistant for officers on scene. It turns what they say into a report draft, and it lists what's missing instead of making things up." |
| 1:30–1:45 | **Translate** tab: speak English, it speaks Spanish; play a Spanish reply → English. | "Two-way translation in ten languages." |
| 1:45–1:55 | **Record** tab: Verify evidence ✓. Then the public PDF with names removed. | "Every photo is fingerprinted, so any edit is caught. The public version removes names and blurs faces." |
| 1:55–2:05 | Glasses view (/glasses): checklist step in the lens, pinch to advance. | "And it's built for smart glasses, so the next step floats in front of you." |
| 2:05–2:15 | Back to you. | "No face scanning of the public, a human approves every report. Code is on my GitHub; link below." |

## Tips

- Record each row as its own clip, then cut them together in VideoForge or any editor.
- Zoom in (or crop) on the phone screen; text must be readable on a phone.
- Show, don't explain: keep each clip moving, no waiting on loading screens (cut them out).
- Don't film real people's faces, licenses, or plates; use the sample incident and your own ID only.
